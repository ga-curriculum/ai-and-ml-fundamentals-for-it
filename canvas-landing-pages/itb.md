## About

By the end of this module, you will have a solid understanding of the fundamental concepts, techniques, and applications of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML). You will be able to explain the differences between key types of AI systems, describe the main components and processes involved in machine learning, and recognize the creative potential of generative AI.

This foundational knowledge will empower you to confidently explore further and engage with the exciting world of AI and ML!
